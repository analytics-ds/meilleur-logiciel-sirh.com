**Employee scheduling software** organizes a team's working hours, tracks the time actually worked and simplifies payroll preparation. It replaces the spreadsheet or paper schedule with a centralized tool, accessible to managers and employees alike, often from a mobile app.

## What is employee scheduling software

Employee scheduling software covers three linked functions: creating work shifts, tracking hours actually clocked, and managing gaps between planned and actual time. It differs from a simple shared calendar through its ability to apply rules: maximum working hours, mandatory rest periods, night or Sunday pay premiums.

The tool is aimed primarily at teams whose hours vary from week to week: retail, hospitality, food service, healthcare, shift-based manufacturing. Organizations with fixed office hours instead use a scheduling module built into their broader [time tracking software](/en/blog/time-tracking-software-guide-2026/), without needing to rebuild the schedule every week.

## Why use employee scheduling software

Building a schedule by hand takes time and multiplies errors as headcount or schedule variability increases. Dedicated software cuts this workload by automating repetitive tasks and by flagging conflicts or unmet rules.

The most commonly reported benefits:

- Time savings for managers, who go from several hours to a few minutes a week building the schedule.
- Fewer payroll errors thanks to a direct export of hours and pay premiums to the payroll software.
- Better visibility for employees, who check their schedule and submit leave requests from a mobile app.
- Stronger compliance with the applicable collective agreement, with every rule checked automatically rather than from memory.

Scheduling software does not replace a full [time and attendance software](/en/blog/best-time-attendance-software-2026/) when a company also needs a deep payroll calculation engine. The two categories overlap significantly but do not cover exactly the same scope.

## Core features of employee scheduling software

A complete tool covers at least the following functions.

### Shift planning and publishing

Building the schedule works by drag and drop or by duplicating a standard weekly template, with automatic checks of rest rules and hour caps. The published schedule is notified to the employees concerned in real time.

### Clock-in and real-time tracking

Clock-in happens from a smartphone, a physical time clock or a shared tablet on site. The gap between planned and clocked hours is automatically flagged to the manager for validation.

### Leave and absence request management

Leave, time-off and absence requests are submitted and approved directly in the tool, with the affected schedule updated immediately.

### Payroll export

Validated hours, pay premiums and overtime counters are exported to the payroll software without manual re-entry, cutting the risk of end-of-month errors.

### Reporting and payroll cost monitoring

A dashboard aggregates hours worked, gaps to plan and payroll cost by site or department. This monitoring lets a company adjust planned headcount before overtime overruns show up on the payslip. Exportable reports also ease exchanges with an accountant or an outsourced payroll provider at month end.

## Comparing employee scheduling software in 2026

The choice mostly depends on the type of shifts to produce and company size. The table below compares the most common profiles on the French market.

| Skello | Shift-based teams, multiple sites | Mobile app, built-in clock-in, workload forecasting | 59 to 129 EUR / month / site |
| Combo | Small shops and single sites | Easy to onboard, scheduling and clock-in combined | From 60 EUR / month / site |

## Free or paid scheduling software: what is the difference

A free scheduling plan generally covers shift creation, shared display and a limited number of users. It suits a very small team with simple, stable hours from week to week.

Once hours become variable or headcount passes about ten employees, the limits of the free version show up: no automated payroll export, no rest rule checking, reduced or no customer support. The paid version adds workload forecasting, payroll software integration and often a dedicated mobile app for field employees.

A spreadsheet such as Excel remains an option for a very small headcount with fixed hours, but it checks no legal rule and must be updated manually with every change, which quickly becomes time-consuming beyond five or six employees.

## How to choose employee scheduling software

The choice rests on a few concrete criteria rather than on the vendor's reputation alone.

- **Type of shifts to produce**: fixed office hours, variable hours rebuilt every week, or rotating shift cycles spanning several weeks. Each case points to a different family of tools.
- **Number of sites**: a tool designed for multiple locations simplifies centralized management of teams spread across several sites.
- **Payroll integration needs**: an automated export to the payroll software already in place avoids double entry of hours.
- **Applicable collective agreement**: some sectors (hospitality, food service, transport, metalworking) have specific calculation rules that not every tool covers natively.
- **Available budget**: per-site billing suits multi-site companies, while per-user billing suits organizations with a stable headcount concentrated on a single site.
